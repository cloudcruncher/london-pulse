    Premises by type

      How to read this. The signals come from Food Standards Agency hygiene-rating registers, snapshotted daily. A premises appearing is a food-business registration, and one disappearing may be a closure, a change of owner or an administrative removal. "Awaiting inspection" means a registered business with no rating yet, which includes recent openings but is not a count of them. "Coffee-named" matches business names only. Treat everything here as a signal, not a verdict.
